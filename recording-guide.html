Instructor Video Recording Guide

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for recording crisp, broadcast-grade educational videos for the AOU Certified AI Associate curriculum.

🚀 Ready to Shoot Video 1.1?

All 4 capture scenes (Architecture, Comparison, Guided Build, Exercise Game) are mapped to exact second marks with synchronized voiceover scripts.

Open Shot List Open Main Workbench
🖥️

1. Screen Capture & Display

  • Resolution: Native 1920x1080 (1080p 16:9).
  • Browser: Google Chrome in Fullscreen Mode (F11 or Cmd+Ctrl+F).
  • Chrome Zoom: 100% or 110% (text optimized for high-DPI stream legibility).
  • Hide bookmarks bar and extensions during capture.
🎙️

2. Audio Engineering & Voice

  • Microphone: Cardioid dynamic or condenser 4–6 inches from mouth.
  • Audio Levels: Target peaks between -12 dB and -6 dB.
  • Noise Floor: Ensure room noise is under -50 dB (apply noise gate & subtle compressor).
  • Pacing: Aim for ~130–145 words per minute for clear instructional cadence.
🖱️

3. Cursor & Screen Actions

  • Enable "Capture Cursor" in OBS window source.
  • Move mouse smoothly with deliberate pauses over key terms (e.g. ### ROLE, XML tags).
  • Avoid rapid erratic cursor circling or shaking.
  • Allow 1.5s visual hold after clicking buttons before transitioning scenes.
⚙️

4. OBS Studio Encoding

  • Encoder: Apple VT H.264 / NVIDIA NVENC / QuickSync.
  • Rate Control: Constant Bitrate (CBR) at 6,000 - 8,000 Kbps.
  • Framerate: 60.00 FPS (Smooth cursor tracking & animations).
  • Audio Sample Rate: 48 kHz (Stereo 320 Kbps AAC).
🎞️

5. Video Scene Sequencing

  • Scene 1 (0:00-0:24): Architecture Guide on index.html.
  • Scene 2 (0:24-0:49): Competitive Analysis on comparison.html.
  • Scene 3 (0:49-1:12): Workshop Builder DO on index.html.
  • Scene 4 (1:12-1:35): Exercise 1.1 Game on exercise.html.
📦

6. Post-Production & QC

  • Trim start/end dead air (leave 0.5s lead-in and lead-out).
  • Add Chapter Markers at 0:00, 0:24, 0:49, and 1:12.
  • Verify audio sync against cursor clicks.
  • Export MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio for LMS embedding.
